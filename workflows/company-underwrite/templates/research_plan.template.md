## Research Question

What must be true for {{COMPANY}} to be a high-quality long-term business, and what price/evidence would create a visible margin of safety?

## Key Questions

### Business From Zero

- How does the company make money?
- Who are the customers, suppliers, and regulators?
- What part of the value chain captures the profit pool?
- Is the listed security a clean claim on the business?

### Moat And Durability

- What protects returns on capital?
- What evidence proves customers cannot or will not easily leave?
- Where could the moat weaken?

### Financial Quality

- What are the 5-year revenue, margin, cash-flow, capex, leverage, and share-count trends?
- What is a reasonable owner earnings proxy?
- Which accounting numbers are misleading?

### Management And Capital Allocation

- How do incentives work?
- Has management created per-share value?
- Are buybacks, dividends, debt, and M&A rational?

### Valuation

- What are downside, base, and upside owner-earnings scenarios?
- What current price/multiple is implied?
- What would make the stock watchlist-only, potentially attractive, or too hard?

### Anti-Thesis

- What is the strongest bear case?
- What could permanently impair capital?
- What facts would break the bullish view?
