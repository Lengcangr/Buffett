# Investment Wiki

This wiki is the durable memory layer for the Buffett research workflow.

Reports answer one question at one point in time. Wiki pages preserve what should compound:

- company thesis snapshots;
- industry structure and profit pools;
- watchlist triggers;
- reusable Buffett-style principles;
- open questions and next review events.

## Update Rules

- Include `last_reviewed`, `data_cutoff`, and `next_review_trigger`.
- Separate business quality from price status.
- Link back to the source report and screenshot.
- Do not remove open questions just because they are inconvenient.
- Refresh price-sensitive conclusions before using them.
