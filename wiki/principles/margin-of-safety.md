# Margin Of Safety

## Principle

Margin of safety means the price protects the investor from being wrong. It is not the same as a low multiple, a large drawdown, or a good story.

## How To Apply

1. Estimate conservative owner earnings or explain why they cannot be estimated.
2. Build downside, base, and upside scenarios.
3. Require a larger discount when the business has cyclicality, leverage, regulation, technology change, customer concentration, or accounting uncertainty.
4. Preserve great companies on a watchlist when the price is not attractive.

## Novice Trap

The most common mistake is saying "it is cheaper than before" instead of "it is cheap compared with conservative future owner earnings."

## CEG Example

CEG may own scarce assets, but at roughly 21.8x the midpoint of 2026 adjusted operating EPS guidance in the 2026-06-09 report, the research conclusion was watchlist quality rather than obviously cheap.
