# Management And Capital Allocation

## Principle

Management quality is revealed by capital allocation: reinvestment, M&A, buybacks, dividends, leverage, issuance, incentives, and candor.

## Buffett-Style Questions

- Does management increase per-share value or only company size?
- Are acquisitions priced rationally?
- Are buybacks done below intrinsic value?
- Are dividends supported by durable free cash flow?
- Are incentives aligned with long-term owners?
- Does management communicate risks plainly?

## CEG Example

The Calpine acquisition, $5B buyback authorization, dividend growth target, investment-grade discipline, and Crane/data-center contracts are the key capital-allocation tests. They should be judged by per-share owner earnings and balance-sheet resilience, not by scale alone.
