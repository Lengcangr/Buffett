# Owner Earnings

## Principle

Owner earnings are the cash that can be taken out of a business without weakening its competitive position. Accounting earnings are only the starting point.

## What To Check

- Maintenance capex versus growth capex
- Working capital swings
- Stock-based compensation and dilution
- Acquisition accounting
- Cyclicality and peak margins
- Debt service and refinancing needs
- Regulatory or legal liabilities

## CEG Example

For CEG, adjusted operating EPS is useful but incomplete. A conservative owner-earnings proxy must also consider maintenance capex, nuclear and gas asset reliability, Calpine integration, debt discipline, and whether AI power contracts convert into free cash flow before growth.
