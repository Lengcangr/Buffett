## Business In One Paragraph

Constellation Energy is a large U.S. clean and reliable power producer and competitive retail electricity supplier. After the Calpine acquisition, the company owns a broader fleet across nuclear, gas, geothermal, hydro, wind, and solar assets. The investment question is whether scarce 24/7 power can convert AI data center demand into durable, contracted owner earnings.

## Business Quality

Quality is above average because existing nuclear assets, interconnection rights, operating licenses, customer relationships, and reliable power delivery are difficult to replicate quickly.

The business is not a simple consumer monopoly. It remains exposed to regulation, power markets, outage risk, capital intensity, fuel and supply-chain costs, and acquisition integration.

## What Would Make It More Attractive

- Calpine integration improves free cash flow without compromising investment-grade discipline.
- Crane / Three Mile Island restart milestones improve without major cost or regulatory surprises.

## Thesis-Breaking Signals

- Material debt-rating pressure after Calpine integration.
- AI power contracts fail to produce visible cash-flow improvement.
- Regulators cap or claw back the economics of power scarcity.
- Management pursues expensive acquisitions or high-price buybacks.
