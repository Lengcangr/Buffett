# Buffett Casebook

Use this as a teaching index. Verify each case in the local corpus or official source before citing details.

## GEICO

Principles to examine:

- Low-cost operator in insurance.
- Float value depends on underwriting discipline.
- Direct distribution and brand can compound over long periods.

How to use: compare underwriting quality, cost advantage, and customer acquisition economics in any insurance thesis.

## See's Candies

Principles to examine:

- Brand, habit, and pricing power can produce high returns on modest tangible capital.
- Growth is not always volume growth; pricing and cash generation matter.
- A small wonderful business can teach more than a large mediocre one.

How to use: test whether a consumer business can raise prices without destroying demand.

## Coca-Cola

Principles to examine:

- Global brand and distribution advantages.
- Durable consumer habit.
- Reinvestment runway and high returns on capital.

How to use: separate brand strength from current valuation. A wonderful brand can still be overvalued.

## American Express

Principles to examine:

- Closed-loop network, brand trust, and affluent customer base.
- Crisis can create opportunity when core economics survive.
- Financial businesses require credit and funding discipline.

How to use: test whether customer trust and network economics survive stress.

## BNSF

Principles to examine:

- Hard-to-replicate infrastructure.
- Essential service with regulated and cyclical elements.
- Capital intensity can be acceptable when durability is high.

How to use: do not reject capex automatically; ask whether it earns protected returns.

## Apple

Principles to examine:

- Consumer ecosystem, brand, switching cost, and capital return.
- Technology can enter the circle of competence when customer behavior and economics are understandable enough.
- Buybacks matter when done below value and backed by cash generation.

How to use: distinguish durable ecosystem economics from product-cycle forecasting.

## Dexter Shoe

Principles to examine:

- Cheap price does not fix a weak or eroding business.
- Paying with undervalued stock can multiply the error.

How to use: use as a warning against low-multiple traps and poor acquisition currency.

## Salomon And General Re

Principles to examine:

- Culture, incentives, and leverage can create hidden risk.
- Financial complexity requires extra humility.
- Reputation and trust are economic assets.

How to use: stress-test governance and balance-sheet opacity.
