# Corpus Usage Policy

Use this file whenever the answer relies on Buffett/Berkshire source material or the local Buffett corpus.

## Core Rules

- Use the local corpus as a source map, not as an oracle. Open the original letter, report, transcript, or user-supplied document before making a source-backed claim.
- Prefer official Berkshire Hathaway materials for Buffett/Berkshire statements: shareholder letters, annual reports, annual meeting materials, SEC filings, and Berkshire-published pages.
- Do not invent Buffett quotes. If exact wording matters, quote only a short excerpt and cite the original source. Otherwise paraphrase and say which source supports the principle.
- Separate Buffett's documented principles from your own Buffett-style inference. Mark inferences explicitly.
- Do not download or redistribute pirated ebooks, paid videos, copyrighted audio, or transcripts from unofficial sources.
- User-supplied books, audio, video, and transcripts may be indexed only when the user owns or has rights to use them.
- For CNBC or other media archives, catalog links first. Download media only if the user has rights and the site permits it; otherwise ask for a transcript or user-provided file.

## Source Hierarchy

1. Official Berkshire Hathaway pages and PDFs.
2. SEC/EDGAR filings and exchange/regulator records.
3. Published annual meeting transcripts or videos from authorized sources.
4. User-owned books, ebooks, notes, audio, and video with clear provenance.
5. Reputable media interviews and articles for context.
6. Third-party summaries, GitHub mirrors, blogs, and AI pages for discovery only unless license and provenance are clear.

## Citation Standard

Every material principle or case should carry at least one of these:

- Source URL for web materials.
- Local corpus path plus source URL from `metadata/corpus_manifest.json`.
- User-supplied material title plus import record from `metadata/user_materials.json`.

Do not cite `distilled/*.md` as the final source. Those files are navigation maps. Cite the original item they point to.

## Copyright Boundary

Allowed:

- Short excerpts for verification.
- Paraphrased lessons with source references.
- Local private indexing of user-owned documents.
- Link catalogs for public pages.

Avoid:

- Long verbatim passages from letters, books, transcripts, or articles.
- Republishing complete books, paid transcripts, or video/audio assets.
- Presenting third-party copyrighted content as bundled skill reference material.

## Investment Boundary

The corpus supports educational, principle-level investment analysis. It does not turn the answer into personalized financial advice. Continue to separate:

- Business quality.
- Current price and margin of safety.
- Watchlist triggers.
- Missing evidence.
- User-specific allocation or trading choices, which should not be provided.
